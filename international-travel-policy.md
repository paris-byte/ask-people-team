# **International Travel Policy**

Since 2020, we have been living in unprecedented times. We understand the desire to travel and/or work remotely. However, for many reasons (employment law, immigration, insurance, permanent establishment, equity and tax considerations), we *cannot* support work overseas for a lengthy period of time. This is not unique to Mainstay, or unusual, for any US-based company.

We understand you may need to travel internationally and encourage taking time off, leveraging PTO, and disconnecting. Additionally, we also have Leave of Absence policies and programs, to support you, if needed.  If you need to take your laptop with you, please check with IT for any security concerns. Otherwise, the guidance is spending *no more *than 2 weeks per 12 rolling months overseas for “work”.

Please note: Travel to Russia and China is not permitted with any Mainstay laptop.

Additionally, the below countries do not have VPN accessibility, so advised you not to bring a laptop to:  China, Russia, Iran, United Arab Emirates, Oman, Turkey, Iraq, Turkmenistan, Belarus, North Korea.

For assistance with currency exchange and conversions please visit this IRS page.

For health and safety concerns while traveling view the most updated reports here.

If you will be overseas, please refer to the State Department Travel Advisory

Safe travels!
