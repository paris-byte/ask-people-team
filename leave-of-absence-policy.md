# Leave of Absence Policy

Mainstay understands there are moments when you’re not able to work due to certain situations. Review the information below for the types of leave of absences (LOA) that may be available to you, find out how you can apply for a LOA, as well as other important information & helpful resources.

If you have any additional questions, please contact hr@mainstay.io  Effective May 1, 2024 - Unum is our leave of absence, disability and accommodations administrator.

**Common Reasons for a Leave of Absence**

Depending on your reason for leave, you may be eligible for multiple leaves which run concurrently.

**Requesting a Leave of Absence**

Effective August 1, 2024, Mainstay has partnered with Unum to be our leave of absence and accommodation administrator. If you have a leave of absence that is scheduled to begin after August 1, you must initiate a claim through Unum.

**Leave of Absence Process**

- At least 30 days in advance, when possible,  fill out the Leave of Absence Request Form.
- Once reviewed, the People team will review and schedule a formal intake meeting within 5 business days.
- The People team will review your request form live with you to confirm all details and gather any additional needed information.
- Speak with your direct manager to review roles and responsibilities and what coverage will be needed.
- Submit a claim through Unum here. Provide Unum with all the required documentation and authorizations by the requested due dates.
  - How To Submit an LOA
  - An example claim form can be viewed here.
- ❏ Notify Unum & the People team when you plan to return to work and if you need any accommodations such as a modified work schedule or equipment.
Dates Matter. If you are changing the dates of your leave, it’s important to notify Unum and the People team immediately. Date changes and late reporting often result in delayed pay.

**What is intermittent leave? **

Intermittent leave is when you or a covered family member has a condition which requires you to be away from work sporadically, either on a reduced schedule or to miss entire days.

**What does an elimination/waiting period mean?**

If you apply for a short term disability leave, the elimination period refers to the time after you're disabled until you can begin to receive STD benefit payments. This is 7 calendar days which means you can use your available sick/PTO/FTO for the elimination period otherwise that portion of your leave is unpaid.
